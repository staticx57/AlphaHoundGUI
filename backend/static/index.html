AlphaHound details & screen
About this replica

The device shows one of four mode slots (M1-M4) and its E/Q buttons step through them, but it does not tell us which slot it is on, what the slots hold, or when a button is pressed or the device is shaken. Set the slot and its mode once to match the device; the arrows (which also press E/Q on it) then keep them in step. Modes 5-7 and G-Force need data the serial link does not carry. Battery level and the light-tight symbol are not sent either, so they are not drawn.
